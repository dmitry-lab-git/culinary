Fruit and Nut Sourdough Pancakes

Nutritious fruit and nut pancakes made with wheat flour and sourdough starter. A combination of dried apricots, black raisins, banana, and several types of nuts creates a rich flavour and increases the nutritional value of the dish.

The pancakes are prepared in small portions using thick, viscous batter. Suitable as a dessert.

Banana serves not only as a flavour ingredient but also performs a structural function: it helps bind the batter, softens the pronounced wheat flavour, and contributes to the structure of the finished product.

2. Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Wheat flour 150 g
Sourdough starter 100 g
Baking soda 1/4 tsp
Vinegar 1/5 tsp
Dried apricots 40 g
Black raisins 40 g
Walnuts 15 g
Almonds 15 g
Cashews 15 g
Banana 1 (120 g)
Drinking water 250 g
Vegetable oil 2 tbsp
Salt 1/5 tsp

3. Equipment and Preparation

The following equipment is required:

  • Mixing bowl;
  • Sieve for flour;
  • Blender;
  • Containers for preparing dried fruit and nuts;
  • Frying pan with a lid;
  • Kitchen scales;
  • Serving dish.

Prepare the sourdough starter and measure all other ingredients according to the specified quantities.

The total amount of water is 250 g. Of this amount, 100 ml is used for blending the fruit and nut mixture. The remaining 150 g is added directly to the batter.

4. Preparation Method

4.1. Preparing the Flour Base

Sift the wheat flour into a mixing bowl.

Add the prepared sourdough starter.

4.2. Preparing the Dried Fruit

Soak the raisins and dried apricots in water for 10 minutes.

Drain the water and rinse the dried fruit with cold water.

Scald with boiling water and leave for 5 minutes.

Rinse again with cold water.

Set the prepared dried fruit aside for the fruit and nut mixture.

4.3. Preparing the Nuts

Shell and rinse the nuts with water.

Scald with boiling water, then rinse again with cold water.

Roast the prepared nuts to remove residual moisture.

Complete this process before adding the nuts to the blender.

4.4. Preparing the Fruit and Nut Mixture

Place the prepared raisins, dried apricots, nuts, and banana into a blender.

Add 100 ml of drinking water.

Blend until a homogeneous mixture is obtained.

Transfer the mixture into the bowl containing the flour and sourdough starter.

4.5. Preparing the Batter

Add the remaining 150 g of drinking water to the mixture.

Separately combine the baking soda with the vinegar. Add the resulting mixture to the batter.

Add the vegetable oil and salt.

Whisk thoroughly until homogeneous.

Leave the batter to rest for 5 minutes.

Batter consistency: thick and viscous, not runny.

4.6. Cooking the Pancakes

Preheat a frying pan and grease it with vegetable oil.

Place small portions of batter onto the pan, forming round pancakes approximately 5–7 cm in diameter.

Cook over medium to low heat with the lid closed.

4.7. Serving

Transfer the finished pancakes to a serving dish.

The dish is ready to serve.

5. Practical and Technological Characteristics

Batter structure

The batter has a thick, viscous consistency and is not intended for making thin pancakes. Small portions, approximately 5–7 cm in diameter, are used during cooking.

Banana provides binding properties and softens the pronounced wheat flavour. When banana is omitted, the structure of the product changes: the result is a flatbread rather than a pancake.

Dried fruit preparation

The preparation of dried apricots and raisins includes soaking, rinsing with cold water, scalding with boiling water, and a second cold-water rinse.

Nut preparation

All types of nuts used in this recipe are shelled, rinsed, scalded with boiling water, rinsed again, and subsequently roasted to remove residual moisture.

Nutritional characteristics

Dried apricots and raisins contribute to the fruit component of the dish. Walnuts, almonds, and cashews provide the nut component.

Banana serves both structural and flavour-related functions.
